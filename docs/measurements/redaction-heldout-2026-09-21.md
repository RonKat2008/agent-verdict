# Redaction measurements, 2026-09-21

The numbers `README.md`, `docs/PRIVACY.md`, and `docs/CONSENT.md` quote for the redactor.
They were produced during M1 Task 2 and recorded in the project's SDD progress ledger,
which is not tracked in this repository; this file is the tracked copy, so every published
claim has a source a reader can actually open.

Two different things are measured here, and they are not interchangeable:

- **Held-out probes** were built by an independent reviewer with fresh secret material the
  implementer never saw, one new set per review round. They are the evidence the gate is
  judged on.
- **The project corpus** (`tests/fixtures/secrets_corpus.jsonl`, generated by
  `scripts/gen_corpus.py`) is the regression suite the implementer can see. It cannot
  prove generalization, only that a past fix stayed fixed.

## Gate definition (D-026, amended by D-027)

From `docs/DECISIONS.md`. G1.7 passes when all of:

1. Overall recall at least **0.95** on a reviewer's fresh held-out set.
2. At least **0.90** of bare positives in structured families caught by a **non-generic**
   rule (a rule that names the secret's family, not `generic-api-key` or
   `local-high-entropy-*`).
3. **Evidence-text** false-positive rate at most **0.02**.
4. **Opaque-token** over-redaction reported, with no hard bound, while standard-shape
   digests and SSH or PEM public material must still survive.

A negative is **evidence text** if a person could read meaning from the span that would be
redacted: prose, error messages, commands, paths, word-shaped identifiers, placeholders,
ordinary env lines, secret *names*, version strings, URLs with slugs, standard-shape
digests, SSH and PEM public material. It is an **opaque token** if it is a random-looking
string of 20 or more characters with no word structure: CSP nonces, CSRF values,
pagination cursors, idempotency keys, request and trace ids, bcrypt and JWKS material,
publishable keys, base64 blobs. The class is assigned from the negative's **category**,
before measurement, by the corpus generator (`gen_corpus_negatives.neg_class_for_category`)
and by the reviewer for held-out items — never from whether the redactor happened to fire.

D-026 and D-027 were both recorded *before* the measurement that followed them, so the
definition was never adjusted to fit a result.

## Held-out probes (independent reviewer, fresh set each round)

All four probes were run on 2026-09-21. "Recall" is the fraction of planted secrets whose
text no longer appears in the redacted output.

| # | Against | Recall | Bare non-generic recall | False positives | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | first implementation (`d1efe5a`) | **0.889** | not measured | 0.106 (blended, pre-D-026) | fail |
| 2 | after fix round 1 (`1f3e65b`) | **0.967** | not measured | 0.066 blended / 0.044 text-only | fail on FPR |
| 3 | after fix round 2 (`408b09a`) | **0.960** | not measured | 0.110 text (all on unseen public-token families), 0.46 blob | fail on FPR; prompted D-027 |
| 4 | after fix round 3 (`2b0335b`) | **0.974** | 0.971 | evidence-text **0.011**, opaque 0.27 | **G1.7 met** |

Probe 3's 0.110 is not comparable to probe 4's 0.011: it counted every text negative,
including the public-token families D-027 then reclassified as opaque. It is listed as
measured, not restated under the newer definition.

### Re-runs of probe 4 (not held out)

After the last two fix rounds, probe 4 — the **same** set the fixes were made against — was
re-run to check for regressions:

| Re-run | Against | Recall | Bare non-generic | Evidence-text FPR | Opaque |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| probe 4 re-run | after fix round 4 (`9f8eadf`) | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.011 | 0.27 |
| probe 4 re-run | after fix round 5 (`a0057f6`) | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.27 |

**These 1.000s are not held-out results.** The set was no longer unseen when they were
measured, so they show that the round-4 and round-5 fixes closed the known misses without
regressions — nothing more. The honest held-out sequence is the four numbers in the table
above: 0.889, 0.967, 0.960, 0.974, with the recall band on the last three fresh probes
running **0.96 to 0.97**.

## Known, accepted residuals

Adjudicated during Task 2 and left in place, because both are indistinguishable from
harmless text by construction:

- An all-lowercase, hyphenated passphrase used as a password value (for example
  `password: correct-horse-battery-staple`) is not redacted: it is word-shaped, and the
  filter that lets prose and placeholders through lets it through too.
- A value that fully matches Stripe's *publishable*-key shape under a key name that is not
  password-like survives, because publishable keys are public by design and redacting them
  was a measured evidence-text false positive.

Alongside these: the redactor is blind to secret formats it has never seen, since it is a
regex tool over a pinned gitleaks snapshot plus local entropy and assignment rules.
